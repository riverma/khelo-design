Actions

Buttons are stickers

Button

Marigold is the primary action; rani is the one loud “go”. Use at most one rani button per view. Disabled goes dashed and flat, not grayed into invisibility.

Chip

A choice among a few. aria-pressed carries the state, and selection also thickens the outline, so it never relies on color alone.
